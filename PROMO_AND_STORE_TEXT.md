## 2. App Store / F-Droid / Website Full Description

### Headline
Experience unthrottled studio acoustics with A1 Swaara, the sovereign audio player built for pure fidelity and zero distractions.

### Long Description
A1 Swaara is a modern, open-source audio streaming application designed for listeners who refuse to compromise on sound quality, visual aesthetics, or privacy. Engineered with Jetpack Compose, AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer, HTTP/3 QUIC, and Room Database, A1 Swaara delivers instant access to millions of songs with studio-grade acoustics.

No subscriptions. No audio advertisements. No telemetry trackers. Just pure music.

### Key Highlights

* **Studio Sound Quality (Up to 320 kbps)**: Stream and cache music in CD-quality bitrate options: 320 kbps Ultra HQ, 192 kbps High, and 128 kbps Data Saver.
* **Swaara Boom Audio Engine**: Hardware-accelerated sub-bass enhancement and 3D acoustic spatialization designed for deep, punchy low-end response.
* **Studio Parametric Equalizer**: 5-band and 10-band audio shaping with bass boost virtualizer and customizable frequency presets.
* **Live Synchronized Karaoke Lyrics**: Follow along with auto-scrolling timestamped lyrics with a single tap in the player interface.
* **Selective Offline Downloads**: Clean storage management that separates temporary playback cache from explicit offline downloads. Only the tracks you choose are saved for offline listening.
* **Futuristic Animated Controls**: Modern 360-degree loop animations, dedicated Repeat-One indicators, and mathematically aligned control decks.
* **Social Story Creator Studio**: Generate vertical 9:16 Instagram Story and WhatsApp Status cards with album artwork, frosted glass backdrops, and live waveforms.
* **Sovereign Multi-Language Discovery**: Native support and dedicated discovery matrices for Kannada, Hindi, English, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, and Gujarati.
* **Cross-Platform PC Edition**: Seamless desktop companion app for Windows with hardware-accelerated Web Audio, 40-bar live spectrum visualizer, and keyboard shortcuts.

### Privacy Guarantee
A1 Swaara collects zero telemetry data, implements zero third-party analytical SDKs, and stores your playlists and listening history exclusively on your local device.
